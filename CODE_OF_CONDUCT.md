# Community conduct

Keep discussions respectful and focused on the work. Contributors of all
backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

- Explain disagreements with evidence and actionable suggestions.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate against, or repeatedly target others.
- Do not publish personal information, credentials or private communications without permission.
- Accept review feedback and respect participants' time and boundaries.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests and other project-managed
spaces. Maintainers may moderate or remove harmful content and restrict
participation. For sensitive conduct reports, ask a maintainer for a private
channel without posting private details publicly. There is currently no
dedicated reporting email or guaranteed response time.
